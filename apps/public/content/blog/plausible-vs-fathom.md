## When Plausible is the better choice

- You run one small site: at 10k page views, Starter is the cheapest bill.
- You need funnels or user journeys, both on the Business plan.
- You want open source and the option to self-host.
- You want an EU-based company that stores your data in Germany.

## When Fathom is the better choice

- You run many sites: every plan includes at least 50, and packs of 50 more cost $10 a month.
- You have about 100k or 1M page views a month: at those volumes Fathom costs less than any Plausible plan.
- You want revenue tracking and an API on every plan. Only extra API capacity costs more, from $19 a month.

## FAQ

### Do Plausible and Fathom need a card for the free trial?

Plausible's 30-day trial needs no card and includes every Business feature. Fathom's 7-day trial asks for a card but charges nothing until the trial ends.

### Do Plausible and Fathom need a cookie banner?

Often not, since neither sets cookies. Other tags on your site can still need one, and our [cookie banner guide](/blog/cookieless-analytics-gdpr-cookie-banner) explains when.

### How long do Plausible and Fathom keep my data?

Neither sets a time limit: Plausible has no history limit, and Fathom keeps your full history while you are a customer.

### Do ad blockers block Plausible and Fathom?

Some do: EasyPrivacy, a widely used blocklist, includes both tools' default domains. Our [guide to first-party proxying](/blog/ad-blockers-analytics-first-party-proxy) shows how to serve analytics from your own domain instead.
